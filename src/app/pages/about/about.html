WHO WE ARE

Working With Communities, Not Just For Communities

SAVE SALONE is a Sierra Leonean civil society organization focused on empowering individuals, families and communities to identify challenges, develop practical solutions and create sustainable opportunities.

Our work is built around the understanding that sustainable development begins with people. We therefore work alongside communities and stakeholders to strengthen knowledge, capacity, resilience and participation.

Through our programmes and partnerships, we aim to contribute to healthier, better educated, environmentally responsible, economically resilient and rights-conscious communities.

OUR MISSION

Empowering People, Strengthening Communities

To empower individuals and communities through education, sustainable development, health promotion, environmental protection, agricultural development and human rights awareness.

OUR VISION

A Stronger and More Inclusive Sierra Leone

A Sierra Leone where communities are empowered, healthy, educated, environmentally responsible, economically resilient and able to understand and exercise their rights.
